Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Pawmodoro ("the app") is a Pomodoro focus timer with an optional shared-rooms feature. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

To use the timer, to-do list, and settings on their own: nothing leaves your device. Your tasks, timer preferences, theme, and session history are stored locally (in your browser's/app's local storage) and are never sent to us.

To use the app at all, and to use a Shared Focus Room:

  • You sign in with your Google account. We receive your Google account's email address, name, and profile picture from Google, via our authentication provider, Supabase — this is used only to identify you across devices and to attribute a Pawmodoro Pro purchase to your account. We don't use it for anything else.
  • The nickname you enter in a Shared Room is visible to other participants in that room. Use a nickname, not your real name, if you'd prefer not to share it.
  • Your live status (Focusing / On Break / Idle / Away) and your recent break-skip count are shared with the other participants in your room while you're in it, so they can see it and, optionally, send you a "Nudge."
  • Room codes and room membership are stored so the app can sync the shared timer between participants.

If you purchase Pawmodoro Pro: purchase and entitlement status are processed by RevenueCat, tied to your account. We don't see or store your payment card details — those are handled directly by Apple, Google, or Stripe depending on how you purchased.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't run ad tracking or third-party analytics/advertising SDKs.
  • We don't share your Google account information with anyone besides the service providers named below, and only as needed to run the app.

Third parties involved

  • Google — provides sign-in. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — hosts authentication and the shared-room data (presence, timer state). See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — processes purchase/entitlement status for Pawmodoro Pro. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Payment processing itself is handled by Apple, Google, or Stripe, whichever platform you purchase through — we never receive your card details.

Data retention and deletion

Room data (presence, nickname, timer state) is only retained for as long as a room exists and is actively synced; it isn't kept as a permanent history. Local data (tasks, settings) stays on your device until you clear it yourself. If you'd like your account or room data deleted, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Pawmodoro is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
